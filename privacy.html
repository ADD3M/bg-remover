Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use this site (the "Site"). We've tried to write it in plain language. It is general information, not legal advice — if you operate this Site commercially or in a regulated context, have it reviewed by a qualified professional for your jurisdiction.

The short version

The background-removal tool on this Site runs entirely in your own browser. When you choose or drop in a photo, that photo is processed on your device using a machine-learning model that also runs locally — it is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server we control. We do not see your photos, and we have no way to.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • The photos you upload or the images the tool produces
  • Names, email addresses, or any other personal information
  • Account data — there are no accounts, logins, or profiles
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or behavioral tracking data

We don't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this Site.

What does touch the network

To do its work, the tool needs to download the AI model and supporting files it uses for background removal. These are fetched, on first use, from IMG.LY's @imgly/background-removal library, served via the jsDelivr content delivery network, and then cached by your browser so later visits are faster. That request goes directly from your browser to jsDelivr — it does not pass through, or get logged by, us. It carries the technical information any web request carries (such as your IP address), but never the photo you're editing. jsDelivr's own privacy practices are described at jsdelivr.com.
